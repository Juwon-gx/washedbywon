FAQ
Frequently asked questions
Do I need to be home during the detail? +
Nope! As long as we have access to your vehicle, you don't need to be present. We'll notify you when we're on the way and when we're done.
Do you bring your own supplies? +
Yes — we bring everything. Professional-grade cleaning products, vacuums, steam cleaners, and microfiber towels. Just provide the car and a nearby water source if possible.
How long does a detail take? +
Interior: 2–3 hours. Exterior: 1.5–2 hours. Full bundle: 3–5 hours depending on vehicle size and condition. We never rush the job.
What's the Extra Dirty Fee? +
If your vehicle has excessive dirt, heavy staining, or a lot of pet hair requiring extra time and product, this covers the additional labor. We'll always notify you before applying it — no surprises.
What areas do you serve? +
Baltimore City & County, Silver Spring / Montgomery County, and Washington DC. Travel over 25 miles from the service area incurs a +$15 travel fee.
When do I pay? +
Payment is due upon completion. We accept Cash, Zelle, CashApp, Venmo, and Card via Square — whatever works for you.
Can I reschedule or cancel? +
Life happens — just DM us on Instagram as early as possible so we can adjust. We appreciate at least 24 hours notice when possible.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 19, 2026

WashedByWon ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website washedbywon.com and provides mobile car detailing services in the DMV area. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

When you use our booking form, we collect:

  • First and last name
  • Phone number
  • Email address (optional)
  • Service address / location
  • Vehicle details and service preferences

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Confirm and manage your detailing appointment
  • Send appointment reminders and status updates via SMS
  • Respond to questions or follow-up requests
  • Improve our services

SMS / Text Messaging

By checking the SMS consent box in our booking form, you agree to receive text messages from WashedByWon at the phone number you provided. These messages may include:

  • Appointment booking confirmations
  • Appointment status updates (confirmed, in-progress, completed, or cancelled)
  • Occasional service-related notifications

Message frequency: Varies based on appointment activity — typically 1–3 messages per appointment.

Message & data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

To opt out: Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe from SMS messages. You may also contact us at washedbywon@gmail.com to be removed.

To get help: Reply HELP or contact us at washedbywon@gmail.com.

We do not sell, rent, or share your phone number with third parties for their marketing purposes. Your information is used solely to fulfill your appointment and communicate with you about our services.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it only with:

  • Twilio — our SMS delivery provider, used to send appointment notifications
  • Legal authorities, if required by law

Data Retention

We retain booking records for up to 12 months for business purposes. You may request deletion of your data by emailing washedbywon@gmail.com.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. Our booking system uses HTTPS encryption and your data is stored securely.
